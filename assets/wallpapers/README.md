# qdesk's own wallpapers

`ember.png`, `dusk.png` and `tide.png` were made for qdesk by its authors, with `make.py` in this
folder: soft abstract light in the colours of the Quvyta themes (amber, iris and nordic). Run
`python3 make.py` to make them again; it needs nothing beyond Python's standard library.

They are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): copy, change and share them freely, for any
purpose, without asking.

qdesk offers them on its Settings screen, beside the pictures of your own.
